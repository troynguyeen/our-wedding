Thành & Trinh

We met back in 2024, right when both of us had just hit the “reset” button on our relationship status. Like… wow, talk about perfect timing! The Universe clearly saw two lonely souls and said: “Yup, it's time to matchmake.”

Our very first date felt like a 1001-night storytelling marathon. No pretending, no playing cool—we just sat down and unloaded all our past life stories and chaos. Weirdly enough, nobody ran away! Instead, we unlocked an instant, deep connection and realized we understood each other inside out.

When Two Opposites Attract

  • The Bride: A Marketing girlie & Content Creator who tells 1001 drama stories for a living. Always energetic, creative, and plugged into every internet trend.
  • The Groom: A DevOps guy living in his own calm universe. A bit dry, totally oblivious to TikTok trends, and chilling like a monk who just came down from the mountain.

And Now… We're Getting Married!

Looking back, we're so grateful we both had the courage to let go of the past so we could become the best versions of ourselves for each other today. Ready for our greatest adventure yet! Thank you for being part of our story and celebrating this next chapter with us!
